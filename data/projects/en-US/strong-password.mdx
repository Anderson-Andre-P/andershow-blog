## Overview

A small app that shows, in a friendly way, whether a password is strong or weak, instead of rejecting it with a generic error.

## What the app does

- **Password strength feedback** that tells the user whether their password is strong or weak.
- **Field validation** on email and password:
  - valid email format;
  - password requirements;
  - clear error messages.
