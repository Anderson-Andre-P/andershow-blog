## Overview

A Flutter app focused on the first minutes of a user's experience: an onboarding that introduces the product, followed by a sign-in screen with clear validation. These two flows appear in almost every app, so getting them right is worth practicing on their own.

## What the app does

- **Onboarding presentation** introducing the app and its main features.
- **Navigation to sign in** once onboarding is complete.
- **Field validation** on email and password:
  - valid email format;
  - password requirements;
  - clear error messages pointing to what needs fixing.

## Technical decisions

- Built with plain **Flutter** and no extra dependencies, to keep the focus on layout, navigation and validation logic.
- Written following Clean Code principles, with a user-friendly design as a goal from the start.
