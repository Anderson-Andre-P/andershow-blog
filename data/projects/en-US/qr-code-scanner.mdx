## Overview

A QR code app that reads and generates codes. It reads codes on labels, flyers and websites with the camera, and creates custom codes to share links, text, contacts, locations or Wi-Fi access.

## What the app does

- **Fast QR code reading** with the device camera, for URLs, contacts, events, locations, Wi-Fi and more.
- **Custom QR code generation** for links, text, contacts, locations and Wi-Fi.
- **Sharing** generated codes directly from the app.
- **Saving** codes to the gallery.
- **Light and dark themes**, with no ads.

## Technical decisions

- **BLoC** (flutter_bloc) for state management, keeping camera and generation logic out of the widgets.
- **mobile_scanner** for camera-based reading and **qr_flutter** for generation.
- **permission_handler** to request camera and storage access only when needed.
- **image_picker**, **image** and **image_gallery_saver** to work with images and save generated codes to the gallery.
- **share_plus** for sharing.
