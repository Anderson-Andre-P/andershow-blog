## Overview

VibraGuard is an asset and service management app for maintenance teams, engineers and asset managers. It brings monitored devices and work orders into one place, so a technician can check an asset's status and act on it from their phone.

## The problem

Maintenance teams lose time when asset data and work orders live in different places, and manual device registration leads to typing errors. The app focuses on three things: seeing the state of every monitored asset, tracking maintenance work, and linking devices quickly and without mistakes.

## What the app does

- **QR code linking:** scan a device's QR code to link it to the platform, with no manual entry.
- **Device monitoring:** a list of every monitored asset with its status, history and location.
- **Device details:** real-time metrics, maintenance history and documentation for each asset.
- **Work orders:** track open and completed orders and prioritize maintenance tasks.
- **Work order details:** description, status, people responsible and action history.
- **Onboarding, sign in and sign up**, with form validation.

## Technical decisions

- **MVVM architecture:** business logic, UI and data representation live in separate layers. Each layer can change on its own, which lowers the risk of side effects when updating a screen.
- **MobX for reactive state**, with **GetIt** for dependency injection, keeping view models independent from the widgets that use them.
- **Dio** for HTTP requests, with **logger** to make network issues easier to trace.
- **mobile_scanner** and **qr_flutter** for reading and generating QR codes.
- **Syncfusion Flutter Charts** to show device metrics.
- **Husky** git hooks and **flutter_lints** to keep the codebase consistent.

The [design was made in Figma](https://www.figma.com/file/ASMDdAUXSnRKAo3ch6vPAQ/VibraGuard?type=design&node-id=1%3A2&mode=design&t=18IICEUM5IEuoqDM-1) before implementation.

## Outcome

The result is a modular codebase that follows Clean Code principles, where new screens and features can be added without touching unrelated layers.
